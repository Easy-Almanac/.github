Easy Almanac keeps one campaign record your whole table can trust.

- **Everything linked.** Sessions, NPCs, places, factions and items connect to each other, so you can see who met whom, where and when.
- **Everyone remembers, not just the DM.** Players add what they remember, and the DM approves what becomes canon.
- **Conflicts get flagged.** When two notes disagree, Easy Almanac points it out so you decide which one is true.

**About AI:** Easy Almanac uses AI in a few places, and only when you ask: the campaign assistant, and pulling details out of uploaded notes and session recordings. Nothing it suggests joins your campaign until you approve it, and you can do everything by hand.
